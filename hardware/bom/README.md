# Component inventory

[components.csv](components.csv) is the starting BOM derived from the available source and device export. Counts refer to devices, not necessarily individual purchasable modules: the IMU and magnetometer may share a breakout board.

The firmware identifies nine bus servos, including four steering servos and five mechanism/camera servos. The manuscript identifies the primary chassis material as 3D-printed PLA with two 5-mm-diameter steel reinforcement rods; separate printed-part quantities and print settings have not yet been exported from the SolidWorks multibody model. Exact motor/servo models, suppliers and prices must be checked against the physical build. Add wheels, bearings, belts, gears, shafts, fasteners, cables, connectors, batteries, power supplies, regulators and protection parts from the CAD and electrical design before treating this as a complete purchasing BOM.
