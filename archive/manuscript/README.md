# Manuscript control variants

These are distinct copies of `actuation.py` and `identification.py` from the manuscript directory. They are retained as experimental provenance; the filename `identification.py` here refers to a navigation variant and is not the current bottom-camera identifier.

Use `applications/field_tasks.py` through the `autonomy` launcher for the integrated task. Do not combine gains or state-machine sections across variants without reviewing their differences.
